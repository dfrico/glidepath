# Changelog

All notable changes to Glidepath are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Three modes: Budget, Runway and FIRE.
- FIRE mode: Lean FI and FI targets, a growth projection chart, and a savings-rate tile.
- Runway mode: budget plan with a monthly cap and a cash forecast with two scenarios.
- Budget tab: month against budget per category, with yearly bills tracked over 12 months.
- CSV import with delimiter and number-format detection; auto-tagging with learned rules.
- Monthly breakdown, recurring commitments, top expenses, where-to-cut.
- Backup and restore to a JSON file.
- Error notices instead of alerts: import, restore and storage failures explain what went wrong, with a "Report a bug" link. Uncaught errors are caught and reported the same way.
- Footer with source and bug-report links.
- MIT license.

### Changed
- Renamed from "expense" to Glidepath.
